Synthesis

Only theories the adversarial critic upheld or downgraded feed the plan. Every expert wrote re-runnable scripts; every critic re-ran them and tried alternative specifications before ruling.

The v5 rating chain is sound and should not be touched: 25 Elo per point (OLS slope 1.035 [0.974,1.097], big-favorite bins calibrated 0.98-1.03), nfelo's QB term (-0.113 MAE OOS [-0.209,-0.019], n=1139) and the team-total identity (beats every fitted split by 0.014-0.020 MAE, rolling-origin n=4363) all survive their critics. Every theory that claimed an edge against the closing line failed out of sample: a rating-based number sits ~1.7 pts from the close, is 0.24 MAE worse (nfelo, a lower bound because nfelo ingests prior closes and PFF; a results-only Elo loses 0.37-0.43), and has been 49.5% ATS vs the close since 2022, so the engine is a pricing tool and its outputs must be labeled that way. The largest fixable error is the totals engine: the spec's 46.0 + 0.35*ratings runs +1.32 pts high and scores 10.82 MAE in 2022-25 (n=1087) against 10.19 for the close; the reconciled formula (prior-season mean blended in-season with K=128 minus a 0.5 median target, Week-1 prior -1.0, team scoring rates 0.30, QB 0.72, mean-preserving divisional -0.85/+0.45, environment table) scores 10.31 (dMAE vs close +0.12 [-0.02,+0.26], O/U 50.7%). The spec's pace (+/-1..2), rain, <32F and rest/TNF terms have no OOS value (the pace rule costs +0.04 to +0.08 MAE) and go to zero; wind is linear at -0.18/mph from ~8 mph (stadium-FE slope -0.21, se 0.05) and domes are +2.0 (FE +2.15, se 0.52). On spreads, the as-built PFF/Cole site HFA (hfa_mod + tz)/25 is a composite that embeds nfelo's bye, divisional and surface mods, counts the time-zone term twice and carries 1.3 pts SD of per-game noise: replace it with a constant 1.75 (1.5-2.0 are within 0.01 MAE; 2.5 costs +0.049 [+0.004,+0.096]), strip nfelo's bye mods and price rest once at the market's -0.15/day (bye = 1.05 pts; the build currently carries 1.2-1.8), and delete the short-week, bye and west-to-east clauses. The QB starter-to-backup adjustment must be applied net of what each input already embeds (nfelo carries 2.36 pts at a first backup start vs 2.66 market-implied); the flat -2..-4.5 on top would overshoot by 2-4.5 pts on ~7% of games, so use a stint table 2.5/1.5/0.5 and never apply it to planned offseason starters. Team totals stay on the identity with the matchup reallocation block set to 0 (cap +/-0.5, mirrored into the spread), because every reallocation tested is noise costing +0.02 to +0.09 MAE. Confidence tags should measure distance to the market as expected excess RMSE sqrt(BASE^2 + D^2) - BASE (BASE 12.65 spread / 13.1 total), not inter-engine SD, which predicts nothing (market |err| slope on disagreement -0.004, p=0.98), and only ~27% of an origin-vs-market gap is real (lambda 0.273, se 0.089).
